HAYMANI PROJECT MONEY v2 - iPhone 8 Plus / iOS 16

Mobile-first PWA. Dashboard, projects (with advance, dates, notes, edit),
expenses, customer payments, per-project ledger, reports (expected vs actual
profit, cost %, customer balance), backup/import, and offline use.

INSTALL ON IPHONE
1. Host this whole folder on HTTPS (GitHub Pages, Netlify, Cloudflare Pages...).
2. Open the link in Safari -> Share -> Add to Home Screen.
3. Open the app once while online. After that it works with no internet.

Updating: replace the files on your host and open the app online once;
the new version loads the next time you open it. Your data stays on the phone.
Export a backup regularly (Data tab).
